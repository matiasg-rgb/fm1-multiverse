Turn knobs by dragging up/down or scrolling. Keys: mouse, touch, or your computer keyboard.

Play

  • Every key is in the planet's scale: there are no wrong notes. Hold a key and it seeds the planet with grains at that pitch.
  • PLAY (lit) lets the planet play itself. REC holds what you play and freezes the texture.
  • GLO blooms, FX adds octave shimmer, ARP pulses the grains, ENV makes keys swell in slowly.

Shape

  • Size how long each grain is · Density how many · Scatter how far they wander in pitch, place and stereo.
  • Drift how much the planet's weather changes everything on its own, slowly.
  • Space the reverb · Tone bright or soft · Presets travels to the next of twelve planets.
  • SEQ adds the lead synth: band-limited saws into a zero-delay-feedback filter. Tone opens it, Scatter makes it sing, Size sets its decay, Drift its glide. XENO, RED RAID and SKYWAY arrive with it on.
  • On NUMERIA a sum floats up: play the key that answers it (key 1 is the far left).

See

  • Every grain you hear becomes a mote of light, coloured by its pitch, leaving a trail.
  • What sounds orbits the planet: one shape per note, sized by its loudness.
  • Underneath, a fluid field flows and zooms past: you are always travelling. Every grain drops glowing dye into it and the currents carry it away; Space and the reverb's tail keep it longer.
  • The body is glass that morphs between shapes. The actual waveform shakes its corners and circles it as a halo; the envelope makes it breathe; the synth's filter envelope lights its facets.
  • What sounds is a little glowing creature whose bell beats with its grains. Swarms ride the currents round the planet.
  • EDIT changes the lens (orrery, texture, geometry, roads), LFO the colours, SCL morphs the body, REC imprints: what you play is written into the planet's sound and the grains granulate it.
  • It evolves as you play: constellations, orbit rings, a tesseract, colour drift, echoes, aurora, the stereo knot, hyperspace. SAVE keeps how far it has come.
