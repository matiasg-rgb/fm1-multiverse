G0 HAN

A little robot with a camera eye in a post-apocalyptic steampunk wasteland under a synthwave sun. It is built from parts and every knob takes it apart its own way: gravity floats the parts off, glitch dissolves it into dust, tape melts it, tempo spins its gears, echo leaves neon ghosts, warmth heats its boiler. It evolves as you play (rivets, a boiler, gearworks, a neon core), opens into little machines, and its eye runs an arpeggiator. Eight worlds: a scrap heap, a neon rooftop, a brass pod in space, the foundry, a rainy bunker, a brass diving tank, outrun hyper speed, and a planet grown over the ruins.

03 / Performance · gesture worlds

Three scene-adapted sound gestures plus a continuous, visual XY controller. Both axes change the same native DSP parameters as FM-1 encoder E2 (grain density) and E4 (motion). Appearance follows the sound-generated telemetry.

04 / Assignable audio + visual patchbay

6 routes per bank · 4 banks per scene · 8 scenes. Each connects one measured audio source or LFO to one synthesis or image destination, with signed depth and slew. SCL on hardware toggles patch editing.
