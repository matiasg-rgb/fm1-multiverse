BALLz

An energy-ball fighter synth with an original fighter in eight arenas: hold keys to charge (a whine climbs, the ball grows, a ball per key orbits), let go and it flies and bursts. Black keys: punch, blast, shield, teleport, quake and a shout. The saw wall, power chords, wah and pump from NERO.

Grain events influence geometry, reverb extends halos, envelope energy articulates faces, and percussion makes pulse rings.

03 / Performance · gesture worlds

Three scene-adapted sound gestures plus a continuous, visual XY controller. Both axes change the same native DSP parameters as FM-1 encoder E2 (grain density) and E4 (motion). Appearance follows the sound-generated telemetry.

04 / Assignable audio + visual patchbay

6 routes per bank · 4 banks per scene · 8 scenes. Each connects one measured audio source or LFO to one synthesis or image destination, with signed depth and slew. SCL on hardware toggles patch editing.
